Pulled Pork

As stated already... Odin does "do" veggies.

Here is a stupid simple recipe for the Pulled Pork

Make sure to following the cooking instructions in the order that follows

------------------------------------------------------

Ingredients

  • 3-4 pounds boneless pork shoulder
  • 1 tablespoon + 1 teaspoon Diamond Crystal Kosher Salts per pound of pork
  • 1 tablespoon + 1 teaspoon sugar per pound of pork
  • black pepper

Recipe

  1. Rub salt, sugar, and a bit of ground pepper onto the pork shoulder. Cover and refrigerate pork overnight.
  2. The next day, preheat oven to 250 degrees Fahrenheit. Place seasoned pork into a roasting pan. We use our trusty cast iron skillet. Toss it in the oven.
  3. Roast for 6 hours. Lucky Peach suggests occasionally basting the pork with the rendered fat and juices after 3 hours.
  4. After 6 hours, take the pork from the oven, let it rest for a half hour or so. Then use forks to break apart the pork.
  5. Serve with some antacids...💩
